# AI actions live in a right-hand AI panel, shared with comments and quality control

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-06
- Epic: #1177
- Amended: 2026-10-09 (#1192), before the first action on a block

The AI tools of the editor are not part of designing an email: like comments and quality control, they help improve it, and can disappear when unused. So they live in a right-hand AI panel, at the place of those two, one of the three open at a time, behind a single "which right panel is open" state and one panel shell instead of three copies. The user reaches an AI action two ways: the "Outils IA" button opens the panel on the actions that need no target, with an invitation to select a block or a field; the AI icon of an element opens its action directly when it has only one, the list of its actions otherwise. Generating the subject and generating the preheader are two actions; the preheader builds on the current subject, and when there is none the panel urges to generate the subject first, without forbidding it. The steps of an action — instruction, proposals, pick, apply, undo — run in the panel; nothing is written into the email before "Appliquer", as ADR 0003 already requires.

## Considered Options

- **A modal per action**, as first built: it hides the email the proposals are about, and every new action would bring its own window.
- **A third panel next to comments and quality control**, each with its own open state: two panels already keep each other closed with cross subscriptions, and a third would multiply them; the comments rail does not move when another panel opens.
- **One action for subject and preheader together**: an icon on the preheader field would then start with the subject, which is not what the field announces.
- **Applying a proposal on click, as a live preview**: it writes into the email at every click, and the subject sits outside the editor's undo.

## Consequences

- The panel follows the canvas selection while it shows a list of actions, never in the middle of an action's steps: proposals are not lost to a click elsewhere.
- Which actions an element offers, and whether its icon opens the list or the action, is decided in one place from the target and its context (active features, subject present, preheader declared by the template), so icons, the panel and future actions cannot disagree.
- The preheader field is generated by Mosaico with no slot per property: its icon is placed by a targeted injection on `preheaderText`.
- The editor part builds on the quality control panel, which brings the panel shell, focus handling and "go to block"; it ships after it or stacked on it.
- The preheader skill accepts a missing subject, and then carries the main point of the email itself.

## Amendment (#1192): one definition per action, before the actions on blocks

The subject and the preheader held, but a third action would have copied them: an action was described in five places, a block target did not say which block, and the steps of text generation were the only steps. Before the first action on a block (spelling, rewriting), the model becomes:

- **A target says where an action works**: the whole email, the subject or preheader field, a block (its id and type) or a field of a block (its id, type and field). The block holding the preheader is the preheader's target.
- **Each AI action has one definition**: its targets and the AI feature it needs, what it offers in a context (apply or copy, what to suggest first), its family, how it asks and where it writes, what follows it, its words. The registry, the session, the icons and the panel derive from the definitions; adding an action means adding one.
- **The steps belong to a family of actions**, not to every action: "proposals" asks, lets the user pick and apply (text generation); reading the email, or correcting it one change at a time, will bring their own steps and their own component in the panel.
- **The panel's state lives outside the component**, as quality control's does: what it lists, the action under way, following the selection, opening a target.
- **An action applied still counts as under way**: the panel keeps its confirmation and its undo when the selection moves, and only says another element with actions was selected.
- **The subject and the preheader each have one accessor**, shared by quality control and the AI actions; the email metadata store is the subject's source of truth, so what is written is read back at once.
